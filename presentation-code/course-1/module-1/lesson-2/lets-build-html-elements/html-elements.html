Baked Pear Desert

This is one of my favorite recipes!

Ingredients

  • Pears
  • Honey
  • Goat Cheese
  • Walnuts

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175 C).
  2. Cut the pears in half and remove the core.
  3. Place the pears cut side up in a baking dish.
  4. Drizzle honey over the pears.
  5. Add crumbled goat cheese and chopped walnuts on top.
  6. Bake for 10-15 minutes or until the pears are tender.
